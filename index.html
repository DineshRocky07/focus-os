Office LMS Machine Learning Cheatsheet

Keep these 3 models crystal clear so you can explain them effortlessly in office meetings (Pages 20–25).

LightGBM
Gradient Boosted Trees
Mechanism: Builds trees sequentially to correct previous errors.
Why used: Extremely fast on tabular license rows; low memory usage.
Output: Risk probability of license shortage.
Facebook Prophet
Time-Series Forecasting
Requirement: Strictly needs columns named ds (date) and y (value).
Pattern: Models weekly weekend dips and recurring renewals.
Output: Future license consumption forecast curve.
Random Forest
Bagging Ensemble
Mechanism: Hundreds of independent trees voting together in parallel.
Benefit: Solid default baseline, very hard to overfit.
Output: Baseline classification or regression vote.
